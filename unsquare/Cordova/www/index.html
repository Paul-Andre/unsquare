Instructions

The goal of the game is to turn all tiles white.

Press, drag, and release to flip the colors of the tiles in a square area of size 2x2 or more.

Use "Undo" to undo the last move and "Restart" to restart the level.

"Par" indicates the minimum amount of moves with which it's possible to solve a level.
